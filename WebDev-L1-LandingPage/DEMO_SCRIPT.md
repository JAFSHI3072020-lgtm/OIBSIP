# AHA Demo Video Script

1. First two seconds: show your full name, assigned track, and task title.
2. Introduce AHA as a fictional premium skincare brand.
3. Present the hero and visual identity.
4. Scroll through the three sample products.
5. Explain the philosophy, ritual, and brand values.
6. Show the campaign, newsletter demo, contact section, and footer.
7. Resize to a mobile viewport and demonstrate responsive layout.
8. Mention HTML5/CSS3 and clarify that the newsletter is frontend-only.
